# AI-Agent-Interview-Handbook Research Guide

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the research methodology for the
AI-Agent-Interview-Handbook.

The handbook should be based on reliable technical knowledge from:

-   academic research;
-   engineering practices;
-   open-source implementations;
-   industry experience.

The goal is to transform research concepts into practical interview
knowledge.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Research Quality Principles

Research content should satisfy:

## Accuracy

Information must be technically correct.

## Relevance

Topics should connect to AI Agent engineering.

## Depth

Explanations should cover mechanisms, not only conclusions.

## Practicality

Research insights should connect with production systems.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. Research Source Categories

## Academic Papers

Used for:

-   algorithms;
-   theoretical foundations;
-   new research directions.

Examples of focus areas:

-   reasoning;
-   planning;
-   reinforcement learning;
-   multi-agent systems.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Engineering Documentation

Used for:

-   architecture patterns;
-   implementation approaches;
-   production practices.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Open Source Projects

Used for:

-   real system design;
-   coding patterns;
-   framework comparison.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Industry Reports

Used for:

-   trends;
-   adoption patterns;
-   engineering challenges.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. Research-to-Chapter Pipeline

Research material should follow:

    Research Discovery

            |

    Technical Analysis

            |

    Engineering Interpretation

            |

    Interview Application

            |

    Chapter Content

A research result should not be copied directly.

It should be transformed into:

-   concept explanation;
-   architecture impact;
-   engineering trade-off;
-   interview discussion.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. Technical Topic Research Template

For each important topic:

## Background

Explain why the topic matters.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Core Idea

Describe the fundamental mechanism.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Architecture Impact

Explain how it changes Agent system design.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Engineering Challenges

Discuss:

-   limitations;
-   scalability;
-   reliability;
-   security.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Interview Perspective

Provide:

-   common questions;
-   expected answer depth;
-   discussion points.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. Research Evaluation Criteria

Before including research material:

Check:

## Scientific Validity

Is the method supported by evidence?

## Engineering Value

Does it solve practical problems?

## Long-Term Value

Will the knowledge remain useful?

## Explanation Quality

Can readers understand the mechanism?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7. Avoiding Research Mistakes

Avoid:

-   treating experimental results as production facts;
-   over-promoting specific frameworks;
-   ignoring limitations;
-   confusing concepts with implementations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 8. Emerging AI Agent Research Areas

Future chapters may cover:

-   autonomous planning;
-   agent memory;
-   tool learning;
-   multi-agent collaboration;
-   agent evaluation;
-   alignment and safety;
-   self-improving systems.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 9. Quality Requirements

Research-based chapters must:

-   explain fundamental principles;
-   connect research and engineering;
-   distinguish known facts from hypotheses;
-   maintain professional technical writing standards.
